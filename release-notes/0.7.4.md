# SiriusMarkdown 0.7.4

October 8, 2026

Recurring prices such as `($2,400/month), ($3,100/month)` now remain ordinary
prose. A slash or parenthesis in a price no longer makes the dollar sign of
another amount close a formula. Prices separated by commas or semicolons retain
their dollar signs too.

Genuine numeric formulas remain supported, including `$2/x$`, `$2(x+1)$`,
`$1 + 1 = 2$`, and inline math followed immediately by a digit. The correction
applies during parsing, so streaming and document views share the same behavior.

Public APIs and binary-package integration are unchanged. Update the
`siriusmarkdown-commercial` dependency to 0.7.4 and rebuild your app.
