# SiriusMarkdown 0.7.28

- Prevent bottom-line glyph descenders (`g`, `p`, `q`, `y`, parentheses, and commas) from clipping across CoreText-painted and AppKit selectable inline surfaces at 1x and 2x scales, including multi-line paragraphs, nested lists, mixed bold/inline-code runs, and tight or fractional line heights.
- Vertically center AppKit TextKit baselines inside explicit line boxes to match CoreText, compute cross-style typographic and glyph-ink headroom with integer-ceiled surface heights, and keep CoreText fallback baselines inside the line box without expanding view frames past their layout bounds.
