# SiriusMarkdown 0.7.3

October 8, 2026

Mermaid flowchart and state labels, and sequence participants, messages and notes,
now typeset LaTeX enclosed in `$$…$$` with the native math engine. Formulas share
measured label geometry with ordinary text. Their glyphs and rules remain vector
outlines in PDF and SVG, including enlarged viewing and export.

Text-flow document PDF exports draw prepared Mermaid diagrams on macOS and
iPadOS. Complete figures fit the printable page, captions and surrounding text
remain selectable, and diagrams nested in quotes and lists retain their indentation.
Export reuses the reader's prepared vector PDF without reparsing or network work.

Quoted flowchart labels preserve punctuation and LaTeX commands. Invalid formulas,
unsupported statements and unclosed graph structures retain the complete source
fence instead of producing a partial diagram. LaTeX in unsupported diagram families
also retains source. A diagram unavailable to the exporter reports
`.diagramsAsSourceText`.

Existing APIs remain compatible. `MarkdownMathEngine.preparedVector` also exposes
prepared native outlines for custom PDF, SVG and canvas drawing. Supported platforms
and binary-package integration are unchanged.
Update the `siriusmarkdown-commercial` dependency to 0.7.3 and rebuild your app.
See [native Mermaid](../Docs/Native-Mermaid.md),
[interaction and export](../Docs/document-interactions.md), and
[installation](../Docs/installation.md).
