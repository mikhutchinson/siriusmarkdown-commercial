# SiriusMarkdown 0.7.2

October 3, 2026

Expanded diagram and chart viewers use the whole canvas. Diagram/Source controls
sit at the upper left, export and close at the upper right, and zoom controls
overlay the bottom. Buttons have no background fill; the active view or scale
mode uses text weight. A soft edge fade keeps controls readable over panned content.

Fit shows the complete figure, including titles, axes and legends, with room for
the controls. Inline figures begin at the reading edge and stay within their
scroll bounds when resized. Native chart zoom retains the panned region.

PDF zoom uses the host's text scale as its 100% baseline. Zoom Out now reduces
both the figure and displayed percentage when document text is enlarged.
Switching Source or light/dark appearance preserves pan and zoom, and PDF-backed
diagrams retain their selected text.

Find keeps its rendered match visible while lazy document layout settles after
resizing, including navigation following a readable chart export. This applies
to document-owned and host-owned scrollers and horizontally overflowing code
and tables.

Public API names, supported platforms and binary-package integration are unchanged.
Update the `siriusmarkdown-commercial` dependency to 0.7.2 and rebuild your app.
See [diagram behavior](../Docs/Native-Mermaid.md), [interaction and export](../Docs/document-interactions.md)
and [installation](../Docs/installation.md).
