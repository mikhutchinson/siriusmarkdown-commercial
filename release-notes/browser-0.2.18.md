# SiriusMarkdown Browser 0.2.18

Mermaid timelines wrap long period and section headings within their cards and
bands. Wide timelines open at a readable scale with scrolling. Choose **Fit**
to see the full timeline at once.

Both WebAssembly reactors include the shared engine from native SDK 0.7.26.
Update the workers, JavaScript, fonts and both WASM modules together.
