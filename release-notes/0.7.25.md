# SiriusMarkdown 0.7.25

Dollar amounts keep their symbols, spaces and Markdown emphasis in prose
such as `Pay $5 today or **$10 tomorrow**`. The inline math parser also
preserves adjacent currency amounts separated by operators and recognizes
formatted equations that follow prices. The fix applies during streaming
and in completed documents.

The package keeps its existing products, public APIs and minimum platforms.
Browser 0.2.17 includes the same parser fix.
